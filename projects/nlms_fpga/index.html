A two-month summer internship at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, in the Digital Signal Processing Lab of the Electrical Engineering Department.

The project

The project was part of an ongoing ISRO–IISc collaboration to denoise 24-bit SAR (synthetic aperture radar) images captured by satellites. My piece was a real-time, scalable implementation of the Non-Local Means (NLM) denoising algorithm on an FPGA. An FPGA runs the algorithm much faster than general-purpose hardware and in a more compact package.

I started with only the basics of how FPGAs work and how to program them. Starting from a basic framework of the algorithm, I learned a new programming language and wrote the implementation myself.

What I took away

Beyond FPGAs and embedded systems, the internship taught me to research a problem, write reports and a paper, present my work and come up with new ways around roadblocks. IISc made that easy: people readily share what they know, and the departments hold regular seminars and poster sessions open to anyone.

Thanks to my mentors, Dr. Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula and Dr. Rathna G N, for the opportunity to work on this project.
